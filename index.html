FAQ

Questions creators ask

How is the "extra money" calculated?

It's the final signed amount minus the brand's first written offer — saved the moment you paste or forward it. If the brand asked for your rate first, we use the rate you tell us you'd have quoted, recorded before we negotiate.

What if you don't get me more?

Then you pay no success fee. On Pro you pay only the ₹999 subscription.

How do I pay the success fee?

The brand pays you directly — the money never sits with us. Once the brand pays, we send you a GST invoice for our fee, payable by UPI or card.

Do you contact brands for me?

We draft every reply in your tone; you approve and send. Auto-send for routine follow-ups is optional.

How accurate are the rates?

They're estimates based on your views, niche, deliverables and usage rights, and they improve as more Indian deals are added. Always use your judgement.

Is the contract check legal advice?

No. It highlights common risky terms. For high-value or complex contracts, consult a lawyer.

Who is it for?

Creators on Instagram and YouTube with roughly 5k–500k followers, UGC creators, and small agencies managing creators.
