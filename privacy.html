3. How we use your data

  • To provide the service you asked for: deal checks, rate estimates, contract red flags, reply drafts, invoices and payment reminders.
  • To calculate and bill our subscription and success fee.
  • To improve our rate estimates using anonymised and aggregated deal data (for example, average rates by niche and city). This data cannot identify you or the brand.
  • To meet legal obligations such as GST, income tax and record keeping.
  • To contact you about your account. We send marketing messages only if you opt in, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Legal basis: your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you contact us or sign up, and for legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act, such as complying with law. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by writing to us (see section 11). Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and we may need to stop providing the service.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run SocialHandler, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Hosting: Render (website hosting).
  • Payments: RBI-authorised payment aggregators such as Razorpay, to process payments.
  • AI processing: AI model providers, to generate rate estimates and reply drafts for deals you submit — only the content needed for that request.
  • Messaging: WhatsApp (Meta) and email providers, when you contact us through them.
  • Professional advisers: our chartered accountant, for tax and accounting.
  • Authorities: when required by law, court order or a lawful government request.

Brands: we never share your data with brands unless you approve a specific message or invoice to be sent to them.

6. Transfers outside India

Some of our providers store or process data outside India (for example, in the United States). We transfer data only to countries not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act and take reasonable steps to keep it protected.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and deal data: while your account is active, and deleted within 90 days after you close it or ask us to delete it.
  • Invoices and payment records: for the period required by Indian tax laws (generally 8 years).
  • Messages sent to us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email with no account created: up to 12 months after our last conversation.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Erase your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us (section 11).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, write to us. We will respond within the time required by law, and in any case within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

SocialHandler is meant for users aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must create the account and give consent on your behalf. We do not knowingly process children's data without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track or target advertising at children.

10. Security

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and limiting who can see your data. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.
