Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

In short: cancel anytime. New Pro subscribers get a full refund if they ask within 7 days. Success fees are refunded if a brand reverses its payment or we billed you by mistake. Approved refunds reach you within 5–7 working days.

1. Cancelling your subscription

  • You can cancel Pro at any time from your account, or by messaging us on WhatsApp or email (details below).
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • If you pay through UPI AutoPay or a card mandate, cancelling with us also stops the mandate. You can also revoke it from your UPI app or bank.

3. Success fee refunds

A success fee is charged only after you receive payment from a brand (see our Terms). We will refund the success fee, fully or proportionately:

  • if the brand reverses, recovers or claws back its payment to you;
  • if the Final Amount or First Offer was recorded incorrectly; or
  • if we billed you twice or by mistake.

Please share proof such as the brand's message or bank statement entry. Success fees are not refundable just because a deal ends after payment for reasons unrelated to our service.

4. How to request a refund

Email or WhatsApp us with your registered phone number or email, the payment date and amount, and the reason. We will confirm receipt within 2 working days and tell you whether the refund is approved within 5 working days.

5. Refund timeline

  • Approved refunds are initiated within 2 working days of approval.
  • They are credited to your original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) within 5–7 working days after initiation, depending on your bank.
  • Payment gateway charges are not deducted from refunds made under this policy.

6. Delivery of services (no shipping)

SocialHandler is a digital service. We do not sell or ship physical products. Access to paid features is activated immediately after successful payment, and deal reviews requested by message are usually delivered within 24 hours. If your access is not activated within 24 hours of payment, contact us and we will fix it or refund you in full.
